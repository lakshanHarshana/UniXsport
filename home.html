About UniXsport

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, accessible, and integrated platform for managing gym and sports activities, empowering students, coaches, and administrators with tools for success.

Our Vision

To create a vibrant sports community where technology enables seamless collaboration, optimal resource management, and achievement of fitness and athletic excellence.

Why Choose UniXsport?

  • Seamless booking and scheduling system
  • Real-time equipment availability tracking
  • Comprehensive user management
  • Mobile-friendly interface
  • Secure, role-based access control
  • 24/7 system availability

Our Team
